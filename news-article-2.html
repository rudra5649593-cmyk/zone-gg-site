Team Soul re-signs full roster ahead of BGIS 2026, adds analyst coach Zealous

Esports Desk 3 hours ago 4 min read

Team Soul has confirmed the retention of its entire five-man roster through the 2026–27 season, locking in the core that carried them to a dominant run in BMPS Season 4 heading into the BGIS 2026 qualifiers.

The organisation also announced the addition of Zealous as a full-time analyst coach, a move the team says was already in motion before the current playoffs began. Zealous previously worked with two mid-tier squads in an unofficial capacity, focusing on zone-rotation strategy and post-match VOD review.

"We didn't want to break something that's working — the roster earned this extension on the server, not off it."

Team captain GhostAim has been the standout performer of the season so far, leading the kill charts with 62 eliminations across 14 matches and picking up MVP honours in the group stage. Sources close to the org say his contract extension includes a leadership bonus tied to tournament placements.

What this means for BGIS 2026

With qualifiers for Zone B beginning September 14, keeping the roster intact through the transfer window gives Soul continuity heading into what's expected to be one of the most competitive domestic circuits in recent memory. Rival organisations GodLike Esports and Team XSpark have both made mid-season roster changes, a gamble Soul's management explicitly chose to avoid.

The team's next assignment is the remainder of the BMPS Season 4 grand finals, where they currently sit first in standings with three chicken dinners through fourteen matches.
